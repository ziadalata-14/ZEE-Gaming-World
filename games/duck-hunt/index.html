Press a controller button to connect. Browser and device support vary.

Mouse fallback: When mouse capture is unavailable, drag on the sky to turn and hold Space to fire. Arrow-key aiming always works. On touchscreens, drag to aim and hold the FIRE button.

Counterattack mode adds cannon damage: planes entering the amber sky sector are marked red, turn toward the cannon and fire a three-round burst after a warning countdown. Destroy the attacker during its countdown, or intercept its incoming tracers. Already-launched rounds stay live after their aircraft is destroyed. Clear the wave for up to 20 hull repair. Practice mode disables enemy fire. Ammunition is unlimited in both modes. Press B to cycle timed shell fuses; near misses can hit nearby aircraft. A shell timer is fixed at launch, with no homing. Headset mode uses native WebXR stereo and motion controllers. It needs a compatible headset/browser and a secure hosted page, not just opening a downloaded file.

Headset setup / read first

ENTER THE SKY IN VR

This is real immersive WebXR VR. It renders a separate view for each eye and uses headset and motion-controller tracking. A desktop browser alone cannot provide headset VR.

1. Open a secure game page

Put the included index.html on an HTTPS static web host. Open that HTTPS address in your headset's WebXR-capable browser, then choose ENTER VR and allow the requested permission. A compatible Meta Quest browser with motion controllers is one intended setup; hardware testing is still required.

Opening the downloaded HTML directly is for desktop play. A plain HTTP address pointing to another computer is not sufficient for headset VR. Local development also works through properly forwarded localhost; the ZIP contains a Windows starter and USB instructions.

2. Use motion controllers

Right controller: point to aim and hold trigger to shoot. Left-handed aim is selectable in the headset menu. Either grip: pause/resume. Left thumbstick: 30-degree turns. Point at headset menu buttons and press trigger to select.

3. Stay at the cannon

Seated or standing, with no walking required. Keep a clear play area and use the headset's boundary system. In VR there is no camera recoil or zoom. Use CENTER STATION after changing your position.

This build does not include hand-only controls, phone/Cardboard VR, a native APK, or a live hosted URL. The desktop game remains available without a headset. Physical headset performance and controller vibration have not been verified here.
